WHAT IS BEYOND THE BITE?

Beyond the Bite is an upcoming asymmetrical, round-based survival horror experience on Roblox. Players take on opposing roles as Survivors and Performers, with gameplay centered around objectives, character abilities, and survival.

ABOUT THIS ARCHIVE

This wiki serves as a community information resource documenting the characters, maps, gameplay systems, and other publicly available information about Beyond the Bite.
